# Tone of the week

- Write for busy executives: lead with the answer in one sentence, then the evidence.
- Confident and plain. No jargon, no hedging words like "perhaps" unless the data is genuinely uncertain.
- Numbers: round sensibly ($1.2M, 14.3%), always give the comparison that makes a number meaningful (vs last period, vs target, vs another segment).
- Recommendations are concrete: what to do, who owns it, which metric moves.
- British English spelling.
